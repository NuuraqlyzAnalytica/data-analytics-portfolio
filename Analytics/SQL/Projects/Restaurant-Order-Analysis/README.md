# Restaurant Operations & Menu Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is an adaptation of a SQL-based case study from Maven Analytics, focused on helping a restaurant better understand its menu performance, customer ordering patterns, and operational data.

The initial analysis was guided by three business objectives designed to explore menu items, customer orders, and purchasing patterns. However, I extended the analysis beyond these initial objectives by conducting additional exploratory queries to gain a broader understanding of the dataset and identify issues that could affect the restaurant's reporting and decision-making.

Rather than focusing only on what customers are ordering and how menu items are priced, I wanted to explore what the available data could reveal about potential operational gaps, inconsistencies, and areas that may require further attention.

## Project Objectives

The analysis focuses on the following areas:

- **Menu Performance:** Explore the menu, including item categories, pricing, and the distribution of menu offerings.
- **Customer Ordering Patterns:** Examine order activity and understand what customers are purchasing.
- **Item and Order Analysis:** Identify popular and less frequently ordered items and explore purchasing patterns.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Go beyond the initial objectives to investigate additional patterns, data quality issues, and potential explanations for inconsistencies in the records.

## Additional Exploration

One area of particular interest was the discovery of 137 order detail records with missing item IDs. These records raised questions about the completeness of the restaurant's ordering data and the potential impact on menu performance reporting.

I expanded the analysis to examine the issue more closely and explore what could be established from the available dataset. The goal was not simply to identify missing information, but to understand its implications and consider what additional checks or operational measures could help prevent similar issues.

This additional exploration reflects an important part of the project: using SQL not only to answer predefined business questions, but also to investigate unexpected findings and identify opportunities for improvement.

## Tools & Technologies

- SQL
- Databricks Free Edition
- Databricks SQL Notebooks

## Project Approach

The analysis uses SQL queries to explore the restaurant's menu and order data, perform data quality checks, examine relationships between tables, and investigate business questions.

The queries are organized in a Databricks notebook to document the analytical process, including the questions being explored, the SQL logic, and the resulting observations.

## Project Goal

The overall goal is to provide the restaurant owner with a clearer understanding of menu performance and customer ordering behavior while identifying potential data and operational issues that may affect business decisions.

The project demonstrates how structured SQL analysis and exploratory thinking can turn available business data into useful observations and opportunities for improvement.
