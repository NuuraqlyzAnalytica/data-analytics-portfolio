1. Business understanding

In your own words, explain what management is trying to accomplish with this report.

By requesting to view all customer and order details including the data that have NULL values. Management is trying to understand what percentage or how many customers are not creating an order. 
They could also expand to see how active the customers are versus not being active at all if purchase date is included.

2. JOIN selection

Why would an INNER JOIN not be appropriate for this requirement?

The INNER JOIN would only give orders that match the customer record. If there is a customer who did not place an order that customer will not be in the result.

Why would you choose a LEFT JOIN instead?

I would choose a LEFT JOIN because it takes all records from the customer table and match with records from the orders table including the NULL values. It also depends in what position the tables are joined.

3. Table selection

Which table would you place on the left side of the JOIN, and why?

The position of the tables are important. Placing the customer on the left side of the join ensure all customer records are selected. The query then performs based on all customer records.
It will then be able to see customers with orders and without orders.

4. Expected results

What would you expect to see in the order-related columns for customers who have never placed an order?

I would expect to see NULL. This means that there is no order record. It would not show that the customer have never ordered. Unless I filter o.id IS NULL.
