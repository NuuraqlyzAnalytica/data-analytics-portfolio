
## About Me

My journey into data analytics builds on a professional background in business operations, administration, and risk management. I have an Associate's degree in Business Administration and am currently completing my Bachelor's degree in Business Analytics.

Over the past nine years, I have worked in insurance and risk management, primarily in documentation, operations, and administrative support. My professional experience also spans software technology startups, media and print, import and export, and marketing. These experiences have given me exposure to different business environments, processes, and operational challenges.

My interest in data analytics comes from a desire to understand the *why* behind business outcomes. I enjoy working with data to uncover insights, identify patterns, and find the hidden clues that help answer important questions. Whether investigating a business problem, improving operational efficiency, or strengthening decision-making, I believe data can provide a deeper understanding of what is happening and why.

I earned my Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate through Coursera and am continuing to develop my technical and analytical skills through hands-on projects. My current focus is on SQL, Python, and R, with plans to pursue the Microsoft PL-300 Power BI Data Analyst certification as my next professional milestone.

Through this portfolio, I aim to demonstrate how I apply analytical thinking, business understanding, and technical skills to real-world problems. My goal is to continue growing as a business and data analyst, using data not just to report what happened, but to investigate what it means and how it can inform better decisions.
